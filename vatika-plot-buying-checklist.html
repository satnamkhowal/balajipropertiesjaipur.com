BEFORE BOOKING OR PAYMENT

9 checks worth making.

हर item पर documents या written confirmation लें। Checkboxes केवल आपकी own tracking के लिए हैं; कोई information वेबसाइट पर submit या store नहीं होती।

Visit the official Rajasthan RERA portal. Search the project’s full registration number, promoter and phase. A similarly named extension can have a different registration number.

Read the current RERA entry, registration certificate, amendments, project updates and any uploaded completion/occupancy documents. A status label alone does not prove a particular plot is ready for possession.

Ask for the relevant JDA/competent-authority sanctioned layout for the exact project phase. Match the plot number, access road and land-use designation; do not treat a marketing map as the sanctioned layout.

Cross-check plot number, boundaries, frontage, dimensions and plot area on the applicable approved layout, sale documents and physical site. Record discrepancies in writing.

Have a qualified property lawyer review the chain of title, applicable land records, encumbrance information and authority of the person/entity offering the plot for sale.

Request a dated, plot-specific written quotation covering base price, development charges, taxes, registration/stamp duty, brokerage if applicable, payment milestones, cancellation and refund terms.

Visit the exact plotted land in daylight. Compare access roads, drainage, electricity, water and site boundaries with the approved documents. Developer-listed amenities may not all be operational.

Get a lawyer to review booking forms, allotment documents, agreement for sale and sale deed terms. Make payments only through traceable, documented channels after checking the authorised recipient.

Before signing, reconcile RERA status, actual on-site development, approved plan, title findings and written financial terms. Do not rely only on verbal promises or photos.

ASK THESE ON YOUR SITE VISIT

5 buyer questions to bring with you.

  1. Is this exact plot part of RERA registration RAJ/P/2025/3745, or another extension/phase?
  2. Can I see the applicable approved layout with this plot number clearly marked?
  3. Which common amenities are operational today, and which are still proposed?
  4. Can you provide a written, date-stamped quotation and payment schedule for this exact plot?
  5. Which documents can my independent lawyer review before any booking payment?
