# Balaji Properties Jaipur — brand starter kit

## Brand idea

**A trusted gateway to better living in Jaipur.** The mark combines a Jaipur-style arch, a home roofline, and a rising sun: local character, property expertise, and a positive next chapter.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Indigo | `#22205D` | Primary wordmark, headings, dark backgrounds |
| Sandstone | `#E7B65A` | Architectural lines, highlights, premium accents |
| Terracotta | `#E76F3C` | Calls to action, sun mark, warm emphasis |
| Ivory | `#F7F1E8` | Soft backgrounds |
| Slate | `#6B6C7A` | Supporting text |

## Type pairing

- Display: Georgia or another high-contrast serif.
- Interface: Arial, Helvetica, or a modern sans-serif.

## Files

- `logo.svg` — horizontal primary lockup.
- `logo-horizontal.svg` — wider horizontal lockup for website headers and email signatures.
- `logo-horizontal-white.svg` — reversed lockup for dark/navy backgrounds.
- `mark.svg` — square lockup for profile cards and social avatars.
- `social-profile.svg` — bold square avatar for Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Facebook, and Google Business Profile.
- `logo-monochrome.svg` — one-color lockup for print and property signage.
- `favicon.svg` — small browser/app icon.
- `balaji-mark-generated.png` — transparent raster mark generated for image contexts.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the doorway opening. Do not stretch, recolor, add shadows, or place the full lockup on a busy photograph without a solid backing shape.
